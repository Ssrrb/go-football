# Source research brief

Research these sources for the Paraguayan Primera División Clausura:

- [PlaymakerStats](https://www.playmakerstats.com/competition/paraguayan-league-clausura)
- [SofaScore](https://www.sofascore.com/football/tournament/paraguay/primera-division-clausura/11541#id:87613)
- [APF](https://www.apf.org.py/torneo-clausura)

Determine:

1. Whether each source provides a public API, or how its data can be extracted.
2. How quickly results appear and whether historical corrections are published.
3. How players, teams, matches, editions, and rounds are identified.

The investigation is complete. The findings, exact routes, compliance caveats,
polling strategy, and field mapping are in [extraction.md](./extraction.md).

The important correction to the original premise is that APF now publishes
Opta-backed per-player statistics as well as fixtures and match statistics. It
is therefore the preferred primary source.
